# Project 5 - Integrate Data Analysis

**Course:** CIVE 202 - Civil Engineering Analysis II | Spring 2026  
**Project Designers:** Arturo Garcia, Logan Baardson, Naylaweh Htoo  
**Client:** Nebraska Department of Transportation (NDOT)

## Project Summary
As automated vehicles continue to become more integrated into our society, it is important that we understand how safe they are and how they can be improved. Our team was tasked with analyzing the Foggy Bottom Data set to determine automated vehicles (AVs) compare with other agent types (cars, bikes, trucks, etc.). The National Department of Transportation asked us to evaluate the claim that automated vehicles (AVs) are generally safer and more predictable than other agents. We performed a quantitative analysis on the data. Specifically we plotted the speed and acceleration distribution and the avg. speed of an AV v.s. a human controlled passenger car. We also showed the accleration and deceleration events and tracked how many hard decelerations there were by each agent type. See below for a breakdown of our files for this project.
